### Project Description
[The "Company Booster" is a web application that uses gamification to enhance employee motivation and productivity. By rewarding users with points for completing daily tasks, it fosters a positive workplace environment through a competitive leaderboard and a bonus spin wheel. The company uses this tool to drive these positive behaviors, ultimately aiming for increased growth and profitability.]

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
[The company is facing a silly, imaginary problem: its "workplace spirit" is running low. This is causing the office to get dimmer, employee morale to drop, and good ideas to disappear, and it needs to be recharged immediately.]

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

The solution is the **Company Booster** app, a high-tech, morale-generating tool that converts completed tasks into tangible "Aura Points." These points are not just for fun; they are the literal energy currency needed to recharge the company's Morale-O-Meter. Every time an employee finishes a task, spins the wheel, or earns a badge, they release a burst of positive energy that streams directly into the office's core, restoring its vibrant, life-giving glow and ensuring the potted plants flourish once more.]
